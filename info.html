Common Play

ethos

Common Play is...

  • A work in progress
  • A collective
  • A experiment in creative gathering
  • A publication
  • A conversation
  • A playground
  • A classroom
  • A community
  • A convening

We define ourselves loosely and strive to center play as praxis and gathering as part of the creative process. We aspire to "make sense of the world through experimental play" and harbor the shared impulse to integrate intermedia in our creative practices.

This is deeply inspired by the Fluxus movement, an interdisciplinary community of artists who privileged the process of creating over the finished product and sought to integrate art with life.

Collectively, we'll develop a "Theory of Play," a working container that documents our vision of what critical and serious play might mean when it comes to our artistic processes.

Forms of Gathering

We'd like to emphasize different forms of gathering - ranging from 1:1 "playdates" to skill shares / workshops to playful crit sessions. This way, folks have the capacity to get know each other more intimately - building trust and creating a community that feels tender & supportive.

Here is what inspires us to do this:

  1. We are fed with an overwhelming amount of information everyday (especially algorithmic), which is easy to get lost in. It's quite easy to be a consumer, passively engaging with content. We instead ask you, how will you assert that you are “here” in the world? Creation is a powerful form of meaning-making & confidence, and that agency should be shared with everyone.
  2. Community feels like a shared becoming. In a culture of hyper-individualism, co-creation is of utmost importance. Playdate is a space that feels mutually supportive. There is no expectation to collaborate on projects with one another, but being in the same space with people who have the same values can feel quite inspiring. Here, we strive for a culture of gentle mutual accountability. We envision a future of resource sharing, teaching and showing up for each other.
  3. Democratizing access to knowledge about making and spaces for creating feels important. Increasing the surface area for the potential to create is empowering. We wholeheartedly believe that you should be able to execute all the ideas you have in your head. We will all engage in mutual skill building, learning and growing with one another. We strive to make access to spaces & knowledge about making easier to reach.

Small Press Publication

We are also curious about what it might mean to center a small-press, communal publication to document these gatherings. This is a tangential project that will be collaborative - here we want to experiment with the book form and with how a gathering gets recorded.

Structure

Playdate emphasizes a horizontal stucture. This quote defines the structure of how we will function: "Community based and community driven output will always remain at the core. It’s less about bringing one specific vision that I have to life and more about being a facilitator of multiple visions and perspectives, seeing how they come together and inform one another. I consider my role more as a seed planter and a waterer than anything else — it’s not about the tree in the end per say and more about the process of mutual cultivation."
